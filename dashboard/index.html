Which protocol launches next — and what is it worth?

Every protocol DefiLlama tracks that records no token yet, ranked by the implied value of its launch. The board covers the whole watchlist and the pre-token protocols below its floor. Live-token protocols sit apart, at the market's own values.

Implied value is this radar's claim, not a promise: a protocol that records no token may still never issue one. Farm plans are a starting point — verify every step on the protocol's own page, use a fresh wallet, and never pay to claim.
